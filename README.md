I am a computer science and artificial intelligence student, working toward practical AI: models that leave the notebook and become a small application.

I understand classical machine learning as a full workflow. I explore the data, prepare features, train supervised models, including ensembles such as XGBoost, and compare experiments. I am building my deep learning foundation in PyTorch — neural networks, CNNs, and transformers (Swin) — through coursework and a course project. Alongside that, I am exploring how language models, retrieval, and tool-using loops can support a real task, and I treat that work as exploration.

## Featured Projects

### [mllab](https://github.com/Ezzeldin-yasser/mllab)

Upload a CSV, train a classical model, compare the runs, and predict. scikit-learn and XGBoost, with FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL, and Docker.

### [clinical-gaze](https://github.com/Ezzeldin-yasser/clinical-gaze)

Deep learning course project. A photo and a short clinical form are read together and mapped to one of six skin-lesion classes. PyTorch, a CNN, Swin-T, and a fusion model.

> Course demo, not a medical device. It must not be used to diagnose or treat anyone.

### [hansom](https://github.com/Ezzeldin-yasser/hansom)

A bilingual text-to-SQL analyst for a synthetic NYC taxi ledger, in English and Arabic. Schema retrieval, few-shot examples, and a safety check that allows only a single read-only `SELECT`. FastAPI and Streamlit, with a local planner or an optional OpenAI API. An exploration of retrieval and a tool-using loop.
